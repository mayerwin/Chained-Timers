v1.4.14 — Ring until dismissed for a whole chain

• "Ring until dismissed" is now an app-wide and per-chain option too: when a chain finishes it keeps ringing until you tap Dismiss. Per-segment gates still work, and you never have to dismiss twice.
• New chains are added at the bottom, next to the + button, and the list scrolls to them.
• A duplicated chain now appears right below the one it came from.
• The vibration setting is called "Buzz cues" everywhere.
