v1.4.21 — Cue settings you can change mid-chain

• Mute now silences an alarm that is already ringing at the end of a chain. It used to change the setting and leave the phone ringing.
• New bell button in the running-chain screen, next to mute: adjust sound, buzz, voice, the final-3 tick and "Ring until dismissed" without stopping the chain. Every change applies immediately — switching "Ring until dismissed" off while it rings lets the chain finish there and then.
