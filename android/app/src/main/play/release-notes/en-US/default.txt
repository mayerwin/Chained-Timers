v1.4.22 — One home for sound settings

• The mute button is gone from the running-chain screen. It changed the app-wide sound default from a screen you leave, so a mute during one chain quietly silenced every chain after it.
• Sound now has two obvious homes: the bell on the running chain (this chain, with a dot when it differs from your defaults) and Settings (the default for everything else). To stop an alarm that is ringing, tap Dismiss; to turn things down, use the volume keys.
