v1.4.13 — Ring until dismissed, reorder, polish

• New per-segment cue "Ring until dismissed": the chain waits at that segment's end, ringing like an alarm, until you tap Dismiss — in the app or from the notification.
• Drag chains into any order: long-press one, then drag by the grip.
• "Library" is now "Chains"; segment cue wording no longer mentions the whole chain.
• Browser voice cues now speak in your browser's language.
• New chains point you at the duration to set first.
