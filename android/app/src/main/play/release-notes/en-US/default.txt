v1.4.23 — The timer that's up is the one you see

• When one chain's alarm goes off while another is still running, the app now opens the chain that finished, with its Dismiss — instead of leaving you on the one still counting, unable to stop the ringing.
• A chain waiting on its alarm reads as ringing now, not paused, in your chain list and in the run screen's strip.
• Fixed: a run screen could show a finished chain's red while its own timer counted down normally.
