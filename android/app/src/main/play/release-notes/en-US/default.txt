v1.4.19 — Alarms that really ring

• "Ring until dismissed" now keeps ringing AND vibrating until you dismiss it, instead of going quiet after one burst. Alarm cues also finally follow the "Ring through Do Not Disturb" setting.
• Fixed a chain that refused to start again after its alarm was dismissed: it showed 00 and ignored Play.
• The clock drops the leading "00:" and shows the last ten seconds as one digit — then keeps counting, negative, so you can see how long ago a timer went off.
