v1.4.13 — Ring until dismissed, reorder, polish

• New per-segment cue "Ring until dismissed": the chain waits at that segment's end, ringing like a stock alarm, until you tap Dismiss — in the app or straight from the notification.
• Drag chains into any order: long-press a chain, then drag by the grip.
• The "Library" tab is now "Chains", and segment cue wording no longer talks about the whole chain.
• Voice cues in the browser now speak in your browser's language.
• New chains point you at the duration to set first.
