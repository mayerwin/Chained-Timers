v1.4.20 — Run again actually runs again

• "Run again" on the finish screen now restarts the chain you just finished. It did nothing at all before, so repeating a chain meant leaving the screen and coming back. The finish screen also counts the chain's segments correctly again.
• Includes everything from v1.4.19: a "Ring until dismissed" alarm that keeps ringing and vibrating until you dismiss it, and a clock that drops the leading "00:" and counts on past zero.
