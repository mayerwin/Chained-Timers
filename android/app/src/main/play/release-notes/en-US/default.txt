v1.3.9 — zero-latency first chime

• The chime, 3-2-1 tick, and chain-end finale players are silently pre-warmed at chain start (volume=0 start → pause → restore volume). The audio thread and output buffer are allocated upfront, so the FIRST cue fires at the same low latency as every subsequent one.
• Routing was already locked to the device you picked in "When a headset is connected"; warmup honors that too.
