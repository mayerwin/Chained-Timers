v1.3.3 — chain rings through Do Not Disturb like an alarm clock

• Segment-boundary chimes and the chain-end finale now route through the alarm audio stream, so the chain keeps ringing when the phone is on silent or in Do Not Disturb — no per-app override required.
• Notifications are tagged as alarms so heads-up + lock-screen entries still appear under DND default policy.
• Fixed the notification-health probe: it was checking a deleted legacy channel and silently reporting "OK" even when the user had muted the real ones. Settings now warns accurately when a chain channel is disabled.
