v1.4.11 — 3-2-1 countdown for every start

• Starting a chain while another is already running now shows the 3-2-1 countdown too — inline in the new chain's clock, with the running chain's chip still visible and tappable. Previously the countdown was silently skipped in this case.
• Tap the running chain's chip mid-countdown to keep watching it; the new chain starts in the background. X aborts the launch without touching the running chain.
