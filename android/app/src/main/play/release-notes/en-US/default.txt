v1.4.15 — Set the duration first

• Creating a chain or a segment now opens the duration pad right away, titled with the segment it is setting, so it is clear you are setting that segment only.
• New segments start at 0s, so you can type the duration straight in.
• Adding a segment takes you to its duration rather than its name.

Also includes everything from 1.4.14: chain-wide "Ring until dismissed", new chains added at the bottom, and "Buzz cues".
