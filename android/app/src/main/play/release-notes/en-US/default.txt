v1.4.0 — Run two chains at once

• Long-press a chain in the library to enter selection, tap a second, then Start — both begin at t=0.
• A chip strip on the run view lets you swap focus with a tap. Long-press a chip to stop just that chain.
• Tap an already-running chain to jump to its run view.
• Editing is disabled while a chain is running.
• Single-chain UX unchanged.

Background lock-screen notifications track only the focused chain in v1.4.0; per-chain stacks ship in v1.4.1.
