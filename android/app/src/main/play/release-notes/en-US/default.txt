v1.3.5 — per-chain & per-segment cue overrides + Android voice fix

• Tap the bell on any chain or segment to override the default cues just for that scope: Sound, 3-2-1 tick, Voice, end-of-segment vibration (and Pre-start at the chain level). Segment beats chain beats app defaults.
• Voice cues now use Android's native TTS engine — works reliably where the WebView's built-in speech engine didn't.
• Settings split into "Default cues" (overridable) and "Other".
